Floor Tycoon · readout for management

What people built when they redesigned their own floor

Seven moments from a normal workday at Applied Materials Korea. Each time, people fixed the problem by changing the office, and every fix meant giving something up. This page shows what they chose and what they gave up.

Filters Everyone · all versions
How to read this
  • Each moment offered a few fixes plus "None of these work". Percentages are shares of people who finished the game (reached 18:00), using the pick they ended the day with.
  • Groups smaller than 10 people are never shown. No names are collected, and no one's individual choices appear anywhere.
  • Headlines and "So what" lines are written from the numbers by fixed rules, so they update as people play. Read them as prompts, not conclusions.
  • The simulated day inside the game (how busy each space was) is a demo and is not used here.
